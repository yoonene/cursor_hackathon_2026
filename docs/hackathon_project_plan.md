# Cursor Hackathon Vancouver Project Plan
# AI Saju Counselor

## 1. One-line summary

**An AI saju counselor that begins with a quick intake form, gives users a full saju reading, then continues the session conversationally by deciding what to examine next based on what they share.**

---

## 2. Problem

Most fortune-telling apps make users choose features manually:

- Full reading
- Daily fortune
- Compatibility
- Love fortune
- Money fortune
- Career fortune

But that is not how a real consultation feels.

In a real session, a user might say:

> “I’ve been seeing someone lately.”  
> “I’m thinking of confessing soon.”  
> “I’m not sure whether I should change jobs.”

A real fortune teller would naturally continue the conversation:

- “Would you like me to look at your compatibility with that person?”
- “Then let’s also see when the timing is strongest for you.”
- “In that case, it may be better to look at your career flow first.”

The user does not need to know which feature to click. The counselor decides what to examine next from the conversation.

Most existing apps feel more like static result pages or feature menus than real consultations.

---

## 3. Solution

We are building **an AI saju counselor**, not a collection of fortune-telling features.

The product works in three phases:

1. **Quick intake** — collect the basic information needed for a saju reading
2. **Full saju reading** — give the user a complete first interpretation and dashboard report
3. **Follow-up counseling** — continue the session through natural chat, with the AI deciding which reading to use next

For example:

- If the user talks about someone they are seeing → offer **compatibility analysis**
- If the user says they want to confess → run **favorable timing analysis**
- If the user is uncertain about changing jobs → analyze **career flow**, then optionally **best timing for a job move**
- If the user says money keeps slipping away → analyze **money flow**
- If the user gives an ambiguous concern → ask a natural follow-up question instead of forcing a category

The core idea is:

> **The user does not choose the feature. The AI counselor decides what to read next from the conversation.**

---

## 4. Product concept

### 4.1 Positioning

Not a simple fortune generator, but:

> **An AI counselor that reads both your saju and your concerns.**

### 4.2 Key differentiators

#### 1. Fast entry into the core experience
A short intake form gets the necessary birth information efficiently, so users reach the meaningful first reading quickly.

#### 2. Full first reading before counseling
The product first gives the user the expected experience of “getting their saju read” before moving into detailed follow-up questions.

#### 3. Agentic follow-up counseling
After the report, the AI understands the user’s concern and proactively chooses the appropriate reading method.

#### 4. Structured + generative hybrid system
- Core saju facts are generated deterministically through rules
- Natural interpretation and counseling are generated by an LLM

#### 5. Living consultation interface
As the conversation progresses, the right-side interface changes in real time and shows what the AI is currently reading.

## 5. User experience

## 5.1 Quick intake form

The product begins with a short, polished intake form rather than chat.

### Input fields

1. Name or nickname — optional
2. Date of birth — required
3. Time of birth — optional
4. Gender — optional

### CTA

> **Begin Reading**

### Why a form first?

These are structured inputs required for saju calculation. A form is:

- faster,
- more reliable,
- easier to validate,
- closer to the real experience of giving basic birth information before a reading begins.

The product becomes conversational **after** the user receives the initial full reading.

---

## 5.2 Full saju reading

After the intake form is submitted, the user receives the experience of **getting their full saju read**, similar to a real first consultation.

The AI counselor gives a complete overall interpretation based on the user’s structured saju data.

### Rule-based data layer

- Five-element balance
- Dominant / lacking elements
- Personality keywords
- Relationship style
- Career style
- Emotional pattern
- Strengths / cautions

### LLM layer

- Interprets the structured data naturally
- Explains the user’s overall tendencies and recurring life patterns
- Maintains a believable fortune-teller persona in English

### Example response

> “Your fire energy is quite alive. When your heart moves, you tend to act before you overthink. At the same time, with less water in the chart, feelings can linger inside longer than people realize. In relationships, you may seem open and direct on the surface, while still watching the other person’s response very carefully underneath.”

### Full Saju Report dashboard

During this initial reading phase, the right panel shows a **Full Saju Report** dashboard.

The report includes:

1. Five-element balance
2. Dominant / lacking elements
3. Core keywords
4. Overall summary
5. Personality
6. Relationship style
7. Career style
8. Emotional pattern
9. Strengths
10. Cautions
11. One-line verdict

The goal is for the user to feel:

> “I actually got my saju read.”

---

## 5.3 Transition into counseling mode

After the full report is complete, the AI counselor naturally opens the next phase.

> “Now you can ask me anything you want to explore more deeply. What has been on your mind lately?”

From this point onward, the product enters **follow-up counseling mode**.

### UI structure after transition

The full report does not disappear. It remains available in a separate tab.

```text
[ Full Saju Report ] [ Counseling Board ]
```

- `Full Saju Report` tab
  - The complete dashboard shown after the intake form
  - Static after creation
  - Can be revisited at any time

- `Counseling Board` tab
  - The live consultation workspace for follow-up questions
  - Keeps only a compact summary of the full report at the top
  - Shows the current reading focus in the center
  - Shows accumulated insights at the bottom

---

## 5.4 Open-ended counseling

After the initial report, the AI asks:

> “What has been weighing on your mind lately?”

The user can respond freely.

Examples:

- “I’ve been seeing someone lately.”
- “I’m not sure whether I should change jobs.”
- “It feels like money keeps slipping away.”
- “Everything seems to be going wrong today.”
- “I’ve just been feeling stuck lately.”

The AI decides what to do next based on the input.

## 6. Agentic consultation flow

## 6.1 Example 1 — Romantic interest → Compatibility

### User

> “I’ve been seeing someone lately.”

### AI counselor

> “If you’d like, I can also look at the flow between the two of you. Do you know their date of birth?”

### System behavior

1. Detect relationship context
2. Decide compatibility analysis is relevant
3. Ask for missing counterpart information
4. Once complete, run compatibility analysis
5. Explain the result naturally
6. Update the Counseling Board

---

## 6.2 Example 2 — Confession → Favorable timing

### User

> “I’m thinking of confessing soon.”

### AI counselor

> “Then let’s look at when your words are most likely to land gently. The flow around May 14 looks the softest for opening your heart.”

### System behavior

1. Detect intended action
2. Decide favorable timing analysis is relevant
3. Run timing analysis for the love/confession domain
4. Calculate best and caution windows
5. Generate interpretation and update the board

---

## 6.3 Example 3 — Career question → Career flow / favorable timing

### User

> “How does my career flow look this week?”

### AI counselor

> “This week favors looking outward rather than staying still. It is a better time to compare opportunities than to rush into a final decision.”

### Follow-up

> “Then when would be a good time to make a job move?”

### AI counselor

> “In this cycle, it is better not to drag the preparation too long. The flow around May 18 is stronger for making a move.”

### System behavior

- First question → domain fortune analysis
- Second question → favorable timing analysis

## 7. Screen structure and board UX

## 7.1 Two-tab structure

The full saju reading and follow-up counseling serve different purposes, so the right panel has two tabs.

```text
[ Full Saju Report ] [ Counseling Board ]
```

### 1. Full Saju Report tab

This is the dashboard shown immediately after the intake form is submitted.

#### Purpose

- Deliver the expected “full reading” experience of a first saju session
- Remain available later as a fixed reference report

#### Contents

- Five-element balance
- Dominant / lacking elements
- Core keywords
- Overall summary
- Personality
- Relationship style
- Career style
- Emotional pattern
- Strengths / cautions
- One-line verdict

### 2. Counseling Board tab

This is the live workspace used during follow-up conversation.

#### Purpose

- Show what the AI is currently reading in real time
- Make the current consultation focus immediately understandable

---

## 7.2 Role of the Counseling Board

The Counseling Board is **not** an infinitely growing list of cards.

Instead, it acts as:

> **A living consultation summary that becomes clearer as the AI learns more through conversation.**

---

## 7.3 Counseling Board structure

```text
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Base Flow Summary          │  fixed top section
│    - compact full-report recap│
├──────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Current Reading            │  current consultation focus
│    - changes by template      │
├──────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Insights So Far            │  accumulated summary
│    - compact chips / list     │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

### Base Flow Summary

The Counseling Board does not repeat the entire report. It keeps only a compact version:

- key element pattern
- dominant / lacking elements
- core keywords
- one-line verdict

---

## 7.4 Current-reading templates

### 1. `general_reading`

Used for ordinary counseling, gentle transitions, ambiguous concerns, or clarification states.

### 2. `compatibility_pending`

Used when compatibility analysis has begun but counterpart information is still being collected or processed.

### 3. `compatibility_result`

Used once compatibility analysis is complete.

### 4. `fortune_flow`

Used when showing the flow of a life domain.

- Today’s love flow
- This week’s career flow
- Current money flow

### 5. `timing_recommendation`

Used when recommending a favorable time for a specific action.

- Best time to confess
- Best time to change jobs
- Best time to have an important conversation

## 9.2 Backend structure

### 1. Rule-based saju computation

- Base facts are generated deterministically
- Same input should always return the same result

### 2. LangGraph multi-agent graph

백엔드 후속 상담은 `langgraph.StateGraph` 기반 멀티-에이전트 그래프로 구현됩니다.

```text
POST /chat
  └─ LangGraph StateGraph
       │
       ├─ router_node          라우터 에이전트
       │   - 상태 정합성 복구
       │   - 의도 분류 (LLM + keyword fallback)
       │   - 다음 에이전트 결정
       │
       ├─ saju_agent_node      사주 에이전트
       │   - 상대방 PersonProfile 생성
       │   - analyze_base_saju(상대방) 실행
       │   - 결과를 compat_agent에 전달
       │
       ├─ compat_agent_node    궁합 에이전트
       │   - saju_agent 결과(상대 사주) 수신
       │   - analyze_compatibility(내 사주, 상대 사주)
       │   - 보드 업데이트
       │
       ├─ fortune_agent_node   운세 에이전트
       │   - analyze_domain_fortune()
       │
       ├─ timing_agent_node    타이밍 에이전트
       │   - analyze_favorable_timing()
       │
       └─ collect_partner_node 수집 대기 노드
           - compatibility_pending 상태 설정
           - partner_intake_requested 신호 발송
```

에이전트 간 위임 흐름 (궁합):
`router` → `saju_agent`(상대 원국 계산) → `compat_agent`(궁합 계산)

### 3. Domain tools

- `analyze_base_saju`
- `analyze_compatibility`
- `analyze_domain_fortune`
- `analyze_favorable_timing`

### 4. Report builder

- Converts base saju data into the full static `saju_report`
- Fills **`chart_identity`** on the report when computing the initial snapshot (English labels for pillar / day master UI)

### 5. Board builder

- Converts domain results into frontend-ready counseling board templates
- `profile_summary` (includes optional **`chart_identity_summary`** aligned with `saju_report.chart_identity`)
- `active_reading`
- `insight_summaries`
- `history`

## 10. Free-input handling strategy

Users and judges may enter unexpected prompts, so the goal is not to support everything perfectly, but to keep the experience stable and believable.

### Main input categories

1. Love / relationships
2. Career
3. Money
4. Overall flow / today / this week
5. Favorable timing questions
6. Ambiguous concerns
7. Out-of-scope prompts

### Response rules

- If clear → call the appropriate tool
- If ambiguous → ask a natural clarifying question
- If out of scope → gently redirect into a supported counseling flow

### Example

User:

> “I’ve just been feeling off lately.”

AI:

> “There is something weighing on you, but it is not fully shaped yet. Is it more about a person, work, or the overall direction of your life?”
